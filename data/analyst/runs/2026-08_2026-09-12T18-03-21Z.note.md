The strongest evidence for displacement: over the year to August, employment in AI-exposed industries fell 1.5% while employment in the rest of the economy grew 1.4%, a gap that has been widening for four straight readings. Because the non-exposed side is genuinely healthy rather than merely less bad, this doesn't look like an ordinary downturn spilling over from a slowing economy — something distinct is happening to exposed work, and it is getting worse, not stabilizing.

What to watch: the prime-age employment rate in the reabsorption panel is the one to check first — a return to growth there, alongside continued strength in non-exposed-industry hiring, is what would turn this reading around.
