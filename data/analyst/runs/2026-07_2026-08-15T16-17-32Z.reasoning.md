**THE OVERTURN CONDITION, AND WHY IT DISCRIMINATES**

My verdict is augmentation, so what must trip it is displacement appearing: the aggregate labour market ceasing to absorb people while the exposed-side weakness stays exposed-specific.

Primary: reabsorption, headline.change_over_12_months at or below **−0.6** percentage points.

Can it fire? Yes. The field printed −0.5 as recently as 2026-06 and 0.0 in 2026-07 — a half-point swing in a single month — and ranged −0.5 to +0.3 across the last twelve observations. The fixed-baseline standard deviation of this field is 0.52. So −0.6 is one tenth beyond the recent low and about one current-value standard deviation away; it is reachable. This is a monthly series and three more releases (August, September, October data) publish before 2026-11-13, so the window cannot close without new observations — which is why I did not put the primary condition on productivity or the labour share, both quarterly and already months old.

Is it discriminating? −0.6 is roughly two standard deviations below the 2010s mean of +0.42, i.e. the panel's own alarm line, and deliberately stricter than the panel's registered deterioration rule of one sigma (which computes to about −0.10 and would be tripped by noise around the current 0.0). Confounders checked against it: the January CPS population control re-basing is the main artefact that could throw this series, and January falls OUTSIDE the 90-day window, so a re-basing cannot trip it. It is a 12-month change, so it is seasonally comparable and a seasonal artefact cannot carry it. An ordinary rebound cannot trip a deterioration threshold. What remains is an ordinary cyclical downturn, which would carry this series there just as readily as displacement would — and that is precisely what the second condition is for.

Secondary: exposed_vs_control_jobs, control_value at or above **0.8** percent year-over-year. Control is at 1.16 now. Requiring it to remain at or above 0.8 means a general recession — which would drag construction, leisure and education/health down with everything else — does NOT satisfy the pair. Both must hold: prime-age employment share falling 0.6 points over twelve months WHILE control industries are still adding jobs at 0.8%+ is a labour market that has stopped absorbing without a general contraction to explain it. That combination is what my verdict being wrong looks like and I cannot construct a benign process that produces it.

If both hold on any release before 2026-11-13, this reading was wrong and should be reversed rather than defended.
