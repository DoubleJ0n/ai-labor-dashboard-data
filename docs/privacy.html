Privacy Policy

Praxis Divergence (com.ailabordashboard) · Last updated 30 July 2026

This app collects nothing about you. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is transmitted anywhere, because the app never sends data out at all.

What the app does

Praxis Divergence displays public United States labour-market statistics and a weekly written analysis of them. All of that content is prepared centrally and published as a set of public files. The app downloads those files and draws charts from them. That download is the only network activity the app performs.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any account details — there are no accounts
  • Location of any kind
  • Contacts, photos, files, or other apps on your device
  • Usage analytics, event logs, session recordings, or crash reports
  • Advertising identifiers, or any other device or user identifier

The app contains no analytics library, no crash-reporting library, no advertising library, and no third-party tracking software development kits. There is no code in it that sends information about you anywhere, because there is no such code to remove.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app stores stays on your device, in the app's own private storage. That is the downloaded statistics, the published analysis, and a few small preferences such as whether you have notifications switched on. None of it is backed up to us, because there is no "us" to back it up to — we operate no server that receives data from the app. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Network connections

The app makes one kind of request, to one host:

HostWhyWhat is sent
raw.githubusercontent.com Downloads the published statistics and analysis files An ordinary web request for a public file. No identifiers, no parameters, nothing about you.

As with any request to any website, GitHub — who host those files — will see the internet address the request came from, in the same way they would if you opened the file in a browser. We do not receive that information and have no access to it. GitHub's handling of it is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you tap the "Support this project" link, your device opens Ko-fi in your normal web browser. At that point you have left the app, and Ko-fi's privacy policy applies. The app itself sends nothing to Ko-fi and is not told whether you go there or what you do.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app will tell you when a new analysis is published. That notification is created on your device from the files it has already downloaded. No push service is involved, no server is told your device exists, and no notification token is generated or transmitted. You can turn notifications off inside the app or in Android's own settings, and nothing else changes if you do.

Permissions

PermissionWhy it is needed
INTERNET To download the published statistics files described above.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To show the notification described above. Optional; declining changes nothing else.

The app requests no other permissions.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and since it collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Your choices

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to request a copy of, correct, or ask us to delete. If you want to remove everything the app holds, uninstall it; that erases the local copy entirely. Turning notifications off is available at any time in the app or in Android settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date at the top. If a future version of the app ever begins collecting anything, that change will be described here before it ships.
